About Me

I’m Ra Tod of RA TOD Musik — Musik Time! I make original music videos and share them on YouTube (@RaTodMusik). My songs so far include “SWIVEL,” “Wally World,” and the tools-aisle parody “You Buy Bobby – ‘BP’ Bobby.” I write my own lyrics, and built that video with AI audio, imagery and animation, edited in CapCut.

Ra Tod's Bus — The Short Bus

Every hero needs a ride, and this is Ra Tod's: a short yellow drag bus, chopped and lowered, built for Musik Time. Up front there's a hood-scoop supercharger and a chromed grille with bright warning lights. It sits on huge dual BTK rear tires with low-profile BTK fronts, with a drag wing out back, tinted windows, black flame accents, quad side exhaust pipes, and “Ra Tod Musik” in blue graffiti down the side.

The best part's in the back: a mobile DJ station with subwoofers, cone tweeters, and an audio mixer with a blue LED control panel — so the bus doesn't just get Ra Tod there, it brings the music with it.

  • Hood-scoop supercharger
  • Chromed grille & warning lights
  • Dual BTK rear tires
  • Drag wing
  • Quad side exhausts
  • Mobile DJ station
